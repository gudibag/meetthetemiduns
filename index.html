Our Love Story

Every love story is beautiful — ours is our favourite. Here are the moments that led us here.

🌟
February — A Thursday
How We Met
His POV

I got her number from her sister that fateful day. She posted her picture for her birthday on WhatsApp status.

Something in me said — call, don't text. So I did.

She picked up. I said, “Hello, this is Goodness.”

We talked. Just like that. I remember thinking — this is easy. Not in a careless way. In a rare way. The kind of easy that you don't manufacture. Those kairos moments you don't plan for.

I called the next day. Not because I had a plan. Just because I wanted to.

Those calls became our thing. Even when everyone was in bed, we'd just talk. Work dramas, random thoughts, aspirations. I was learning her without even trying. And somewhere in those midnight conversations, without making any announcements about it, I knew she was going to be important to me.

When we finally met in person it confirmed what I already felt. She was exactly herself. No performance. Just her. And that peace — that's the thing I can't fully explain. Being around her just settles something in me.

There were beautiful seasons. There were hard ones too. But God kept showing up in the middle of all of it, threading things together in ways none of us could have scripted.

When it was time, I said it plainly. No games. No hints. Just this is who I am, this is what I want, and I want it with you.

She said yes. And honestly, everything since has just felt like grace.

From a birthday call I almost didn't make to a lifetime I can't imagine any other way.

God can be trusted to finish what he starts.

Her POV

It’s a special Thursday evening, sometime in February, which was my birthday. I was receiving calls from family and friends, with gifts. Then my phone rings and a deep voice says “Hello, this is Goodness.” And I was thinking “Goodness? That’s a name, not a greeting 😂”

But then we talk. And talk. About random things, real things, dreams. No awkward silences, just… ease. The next day he calls again. Then again. Those short “birthday check-ins” turned into our daily 12am conversations. Months of just getting to know each other’s hearts before he ever asked me out.

Later on, our online conversations led to us meeting physically now and then through our busy schedules, and it’s been wonderful.

Then he finally said his intentions straight, no games. Just honest and safe at the same time.

There were sweet moments. There were ups and downs. But through it all, God kept weaving us together.

A year later, heart pounding as he proposed. From “this is Goodness” to “will you marry me” — and I gave a sweet “yes”.

Now by His grace, we’re becoming one in the love. A love that starts with a birthday call and ends at the altar… God is really wonderful.

Common Questions

What is the dress code?+
For the Traditional Wedding, we encourage guests to wear Aso-Ebi or elegant traditional Nigerian attire. For the White Wedding, the dress code is formal or semi-formal. Details on Aso-Ebi fabric will be shared separately.
Can I bring children?+
Children are warmly welcome! Please include them in your guest count when you RSVP so we can prepare adequately. We'll have a designated family-friendly area.
Is there parking available?+
Yes, parking will be available at the venue. [Add specific parking details here once confirmed with the venue.] We also encourage car-pooling and will coordinate guest shuttles from key pickup points.
Will there be a live stream?+
Yes! We know not everyone can make it in person, so we plan to live stream the White Wedding ceremony. Details on the streaming link will be shared closer to the date on our social media pages.
What time should I arrive?+
We kindly ask all guests to arrive 30–45 minutes before the ceremony start time. This gives us time to seat everyone before proceedings begin. Nigerian time is beautiful, but let's make an exception for this one! 😄
How do I confirm my RSVP?+
Simply fill out the RSVP form above on this page. You'll receive a confirmation and your response will be saved. If you encounter any issues, please reach out to us on WhatsApp via the link in the footer.
